The credit-readiness path

Our training runs in this order, for a reason.
Step 1

Record every shilling

Start a daily ledger of sales, costs, and stock — the raw record every later step is built on.

Step 2

Build a track record

Keep it up for a set period so there's a consistent history to show, not just one good month.

Step 3

Meet the requirements

Learn exactly what banks and lenders ask for — documents, statements, guarantors — and get them in order.

Step 4

Open accounts & apply

Open a business bank account, set up mobile money for the business, and apply for credit with confidence.
